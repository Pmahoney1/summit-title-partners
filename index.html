What We Do

We Solve What Others Walk Away From

Complex property situations require more than a quick offer. We investigate what's actually happening, identify the realistic options, and help you understand each one.

Curative Title

We resolve clouds on title — tax liens, missing heirs, recording errors, and chain-of-title breaks that prevent you from selling, refinancing, or keeping your property.

  • Tax lien analysis & redemption
  • Chain of title correction
  • Recording error resolution

Delinquent Property Tax Options

If you're behind on payments or received a notice, we explain the actual deadlines and what they mean, then walk you through every realistic path forward — including ones that don't involve selling.

  • Understand your timeline & deadlines
  • Keep, stay, sell, or exit options
  • Potential tax relief loans

Inherited Property

When an owner passes without proper probate, the property can be stuck. We work through heirship research, succession, and heir coordination to establish clear ownership and a path forward for the family.

  • Heir identification & outreach
  • Intestate succession research
  • Probate process coordination

Liens, Taxes & Judgments

Years of delinquent property taxes, competing liens, or judgments can make a property feel impossible to deal with. We untangle the claims, negotiate where possible, and coordinate with attorneys as needed.

  • Delinquent tax analysis & redemption
  • Multi-lien priority analysis
  • Judgment lien negotiation

Property Investigation

Before recommending anything, we investigate — ownership, value, mortgages, taxes, liens, foreclosure status, heirs, code issues, and deadlines — so you understand what is actually happening with your property.

  • Full ownership & title research
  • Lien, tax & judgment discovery
  • Clear summary of what we found

Flexible Solutions

Not every situation calls for a sale. Depending on your needs, the path may involve keeping the property, curing the issue, selling with time to relocate, a creative structure, or a direct purchase.

  • Keep or stay in your home
  • Sell on your timeline
  • Creative & direct purchase options

How We Work

Understand First,
Recommend Second.

We don't lead with an offer. We follow the same disciplined process on every situation, so you understand your options before you decide.

01

Listen & Understand

Before we talk about solutions, we learn what happened, what you're concerned about, what you've already tried, and what a good outcome looks like to you. Two identical properties can need completely different answers.

02

Investigate the Property

We research what's actually happening — ownership, value, mortgages, taxes, liens, judgments, foreclosure status, title, heirs, and important deadlines. We investigate before we prescribe.

03

Identify & Explain Your Options

We combine what you want with what the property allows and what can realistically be executed, then walk you through each viable path and what it would mean for you. The decision is yours.

04

Execute

Once you choose a path, we get it done — coordinating escrow, title, attorneys, lenders, heirs, and buyers as needed. Our reputation rests on doing what we said we would do.
