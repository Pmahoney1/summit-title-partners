What We Do

Property Problems We Solve in California

Complex property situations require more than a quick offer. We investigate what's actually happening, identify the realistic options, and help you understand each one.

Behind on Property Taxes

If you're behind on payments or received a notice from the county, we explain the actual deadlines and what they mean, then walk you through every realistic path forward, including ones that don't involve selling.

  • Understand your timeline & deadlines
  • Keep, stay, sell, or exit options
  • Potential tax relief loans

Inherited a House

When an owner passes without proper probate, the property can be stuck. We work through heirship research, succession, and heir coordination to establish clear ownership and a path forward for the family.

  • Heir identification & outreach
  • Intestate succession research
  • Probate process coordination

Liens, Taxes and Judgments

Years of delinquent property taxes, competing liens, or judgments can make a property feel impossible to deal with. We untangle the claims, negotiate where possible, and coordinate with attorneys as needed.

  • Delinquent tax analysis & redemption
  • Multi-lien priority analysis
  • Judgment lien negotiation

Title Problems

We resolve clouds on title: tax liens, missing heirs, recording errors, and chain-of-title breaks that prevent you from selling, refinancing, or keeping your property.

  • Tax lien analysis & redemption
  • Chain-of-title correction
  • Recording error resolution

How We Work

Understand first, recommend second.

We don't lead with an offer. We follow the same disciplined process on every situation, so you understand your options before you decide.

01

Listen & Understand

Before we talk about solutions, we learn what happened, what you're concerned about, what you've already tried, and what a good outcome looks like to you. Two identical properties can need completely different answers.

02

Investigate the Property

We research what's actually happening: ownership, value, mortgages, taxes, liens, judgments, title, heirs, and important deadlines. We investigate before we prescribe.

Answers

Common Questions

Do I have to sell my house?

No. Many of the situations we work on end without a sale. Depending on the property, the realistic paths may include a payment plan with the county, a tax relief loan, curing a title defect so you can refinance or stay, or a creative structure. We lay out every option, including the ones that do not involve us, and the decision is yours.

How fast can you respond?

Within one business day. If a county deadline is close, say so in the form and we will prioritize it. We explain the real deadlines rather than manufacture urgency.

Are you a title company or attorneys?

No. Summit Title Partners is a private real estate solutions company. We are not a law firm, title or escrow company, government agency, or nonprofit. We investigate, explain your options, and coordinate with licensed attorneys and title and escrow professionals when a path needs them. Patrick Mahoney is a licensed California real estate agent, DRE 02209290.

What does the consultation cost?

Nothing. The conversation, the property investigation, and the written summary of your options are free, with no obligation. If a solution involves a real estate transaction we participate in, we may earn from that transaction, and we tell you so up front.

Which parts of California do you serve?

Every California county. We are based in Sacramento and work with homeowners across the state, including Sacramento County and Sonoma County, the Bay Area, the Central Valley, the North Coast, and Southern California.

What happens after I submit the form?

Patrick or Trent reads it and contacts you within one business day. We start by listening to what happened and what you want. Then we investigate the property records: ownership, taxes, liens, judgments, heirs, and deadlines. You receive a plain-English written summary of what we found and your realistic options, usually within about a week.

Can you help if the property is already on the county tax sale list?

Often, yes, but time matters. In California the right to redeem a tax-defaulted property generally runs until the close of business on the last business day before the tax sale. Up to that point, paths may include redemption, a tax relief loan, or a sale on a timeline that lets you keep your equity. Contact us as early as you can so there is time to investigate and act.

What if there are multiple heirs?

That is common, and it is something we work through regularly. We identify and reach every heir, research the order of intestate succession under the California Probate Code when there is no will, and coordinate probate or a small estate procedure where one is needed. Every heir's share and signature matters, and we help the family reach a decision together.
