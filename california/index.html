California Homeowners

Where We Work in California

Property problems in California follow state law, but the work happens at the county level. Each county has its own tax collector, recorder, assessor, and probate court, its own tax-sale schedule, and its own way of searching recorded documents. Knowing which office holds which record, and what that county's deadlines look like, is a large part of solving any of these problems.

Summit Title Partners helps California homeowners, heirs, and families with property taxes, inherited property, liens and judgments, and title problems in every county in the state. Our county pages list the local offices and how things work there. Where we have not published a page for your county yet, the free property investigation covers it just the same.
